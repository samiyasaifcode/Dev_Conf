Frequently Asked Questions

Are workshop materials included?

Yes! All Pro and Team ticket holders get lifetime access to workshop materials, while Standard gets 30-day digital access.

Can I switch my ticket type later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade your Standard ticket to Pro at any time before the conference, subject to availability.

Is there a virtual attendance option?

We offer a digital-only pass that includes live streams of all main stage keynotes and access to our virtual networking lounge.

What is the refund policy?

Tickets are fully refundable up to 30 days before the event. After that, you can transfer your ticket to a colleague for free.
